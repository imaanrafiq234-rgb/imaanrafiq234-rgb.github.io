Wind Turbine

Rotor study, built at full size

2023Group build1 image

A small horizontal axis turbine, built and set up on the Aston campus lawn, with a CFD study behind the rotor.

Two builds went up side by side: a three blade rotor folded from sheet aluminium on a steel tripod, and a shorter set of blades on an orange hub. The CFD work looked at how the wind behaves across the blade sections before the geometry was cut, which is why the blades stay flat and simple rather than styled.

LOOM

End-to-end e-commerce UX and UI

2025Digital product design6 images

A functioning e-commerce experience for considered wardrobe and home goods, built as a sandbox for the whole UX and UI process.

Plotting a six-step customer journey early, and roughly, surfaced the friction before any visual design was committed. The interface was then assembled from Auto Layout components and variants.

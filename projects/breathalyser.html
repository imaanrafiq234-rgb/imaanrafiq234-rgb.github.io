Breathalyser

Sketch, CAD, render

2024Product design3 images

A handheld alcohol breathalyser through the full pipeline, from red-line concepts to parametric CAD and a high-fidelity render.

The handle tapers to 28 mm at the palm, and the mouthpiece axis carries a 15 mm offset so the display holds a 45-degree sight line while the user blows.

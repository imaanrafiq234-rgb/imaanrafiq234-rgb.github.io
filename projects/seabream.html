Sea Beam

Modular marine searchlight

2025Product design · Industry brief3 images

A modular searchlight for offshore platforms, rescue vessels and marina work, designed with the Lighting Industry Association and HoloPhane.

The housing is 6061-T6 aluminium, CNC milled and powder coated, with borosilicate glass over twin LED optics. A micro wind turbine and solar backup feed a 24 V, 20 Ah LiFePO4 pack, which holds about 12 hours of backup at 40 W. Every internal part is a replaceable module, because offshore the harder problem is not the light, it is getting someone out to fix it.

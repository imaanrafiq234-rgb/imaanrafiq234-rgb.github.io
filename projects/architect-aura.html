Architect Aura

Build, then wear test

2025Footwear concept3 images

A trail running sneaker concept carried from a shop page mockup into a study of how the sole actually wears.

The build is a mesh and TPU upper on a dual density EVA midsole with a Vibram outsole, 385 g a shoe. The sketch sheet works the wear rather than the styling: pressure maps under the forefoot, tension lines across the toe box, and the roll from heel strike through mid stance to toe off.

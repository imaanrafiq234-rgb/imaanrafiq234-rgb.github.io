Botanic Rituals

Limited apothecary batch, structure and volumetrics

2025Packaging design1 image

A limited apothecary batch of cold-pressed botanic seed oil, with packaging structure, volumetrics and a swatch reference.

The carton was worked as a volumetrics study: how much air can the pack hold before it reads as waste on a shelf? A pouch and batch card make unboxing the first step of the routine.

Portfolio Deck

The printed book

2025Self-initiated4 images

The printed book behind four of the projects here. Its spreads now sit inside each project, so the work can be read on its own rather than as one long sequence.

Each project follows the same rhythm: title, brief, research and insight, process, outcome. Holding that structure steady lets the work carry the variation. What is left here is the book itself: the cover, the two opening pages and a contact sheet of renders, with the project spreads now filed under their own projects.

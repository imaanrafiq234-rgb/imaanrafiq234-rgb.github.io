Aura

Ultrasonic microcurrent facial contouring system

2025Ergonomic wellness hardware1 image

High-precision skincare hardware with capacitive skin-contact gating, 3 MHz ultrasonic infusion and biosafe anodised champagne gold electrode nodes.

The device only energises its electrodes when capacitive sensing confirms real skin contact. That one behaviour is what separates a wellness tool from a hazard, and it is invisible in the object.

Cobra Coffee Machine

Form, flow and internal architecture

2025Product design16 images

A start-to-finish development of a modern coffee machine, with a design language taken from the posture of a cobra.

The real work was inside: fitting pumps, heating elements, reservoir and tubing into a tapered organic form. Two competing architectures were settled on paper and in 1:1 clay before any CAD time was spent.

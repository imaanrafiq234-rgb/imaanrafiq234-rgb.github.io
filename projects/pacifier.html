Cocomelon Pacifier

Child safety, materials and circular economy

2025Product design6 images

A pacifier developed around a single-mum persona, where the material choice decided the product rather than the styling.

Silicone was specified for heat resistance, flexibility and repeat sterilising. Colour carries meaning rather than decoration: green for calm, cream for warmth, red to catch a parent’s eye, blue for trust.

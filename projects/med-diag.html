Med-Diag X1

Handheld biometric triage platform

2025Medical hardware1 image

A non-invasive clinical diagnostic device combining PPG pulse monitoring, contactless infrared thermopiles and a thumb-wheel interface for field triage.

Field triage is a one-handed job. The thumb wheel was placed by measuring the single-handed thumb sweep across the 5th to 95th percentile reach, with thermal cutaways keeping the electronics inside safe limits.

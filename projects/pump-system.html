Pump System

The paper trail behind the product

2025Hardware engineering7 images

The engineering paper trail for a pressure pump system: geometry sketches, block diagram, circuit schematic, sizing calculations and CAD.

At 12 V dc and 10 A maximum the system draws 120 W, and 30 W at a typical 2.5 A. Sizing a 1:7 divider and amplifier gains of 50 and 2 turned the numbers into a circuit that could actually be built.

Pizza Oven

Sketch, mockup, render

2025Concept study7 images

A pizza oven concept developed sketch first, then proven in cardboard, then refined in render.

The chimney manages airflow so the oven does not overheat, and the side hole loads and clears coal with a shovel. Walnut veneer body, powder-coated ends and castors for moving it.

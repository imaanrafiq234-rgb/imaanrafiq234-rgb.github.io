Khan Terminal Interview Academy

A week one audit, then a redesign proposal for the academy

2026Product and UX review8 images

Read the full Khan Terminal case study →

A week one accessibility and UX audit of a live finance interview-prep platform, followed by eight screen concepts for the Interview Academy.

The audit found seven issues, three of them blocking: a greeting that used a demo name, a watchlist that returned a 404, and a header that still asked a signed-in user to sign in. The proposal keeps every engine and rebuilds the surface around one clear next action.
